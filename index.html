Basement Gaming Setup

A Proposal for Improving the Basement Space

Budget: $500–$1,000 • Style: Dark/Black • Purpose: PC Gaming + Reading

Overview

The basement currently has a large indoor basketball arcade machine taking up significant space in the left corner of the room. This proposal outlines a plan to remove the basketball hoop and replace it with a functional, organized gaming and reading setup — making the basement a multi-purpose space the whole family can enjoy.

The Problem

  • The basketball arcade machine occupies the entire left corner of the room

  • The space is underutilized and difficult to navigate around

  • There is no dedicated workspace or comfortable gaming area

  • Books and accessories are on a small tree-style shelf with limited capacity



The Proposed Solution

Replace the basketball hoop with a clean, organized gaming setup built around five key items:

1. Sceptre 34" Curved Ultrawide WQHD Monitor (C345B Series)

A 34-inch curved ultrawide monitor with 3440x1440 WQHD resolution, up to 180Hz refresh rate, 1ms response time, R1500 curvature, AMD FreeSync Premium, 2x DisplayPort, 99% sRGB, and built-in speakers. Delivers 30% more screen space than a standard monitor — ideal for both gaming and productivity.

Product link: amazon.com/dp/B0F3BBBPXN

2. WALI Heavy Duty Gas Spring Monitor Arm (GSM001XLN)

A heavy-duty gas spring arm supporting 17-49" screens up to 33 lbs. Zero-drift stability, full tilt/swivel/rotate, C-clamp and grommet base included. Frees up over 80% of desk surface and positions the ultrawide at perfect eye level.

Product link: amazon.com/dp/B0FRRCGF2Q

3. PXN V99 Force Feedback Racing Wheel with Pedals & Shifter

A complete sim racing setup in one package: 3.2Nm dual-motor force feedback, 11.8-inch detachable wheel with TPR rubber grip, adjustable 270°/900° rotation, Hall-effect 3-pedal set (throttle, brake, clutch), 6+1 H-pattern shifter, and 4 paddle shifters. Compatible with PC, PS4, Xbox One, and Xbox Series X|S. Supports Assetto Corsa, F1, Dirt Rally, BeamNG.drive, and more. App-enabled tuning via the PXN WHEEL app.

Product link: amazon.com/dp/B0D6R22N4V

4. SHW Vista 51" L-Shaped Desk

An L-shaped desk that fits snugly into the top-left corner where the basketball hoop currently sits. Features a 51" x 51" surface, built-in monitor shelf, pull-out storage drawer, adjustable leveling glides, and a durable steel frame with laminated wood top.

Product link: amazon.com/dp/B09156SFG2

5. IKEA BILLY Bookcase (31½ x 11 x 79½")

A tall classic bookcase placed along the left wall below the desk. Nearly 80" of height with adjustable shelves for books, games, and accessories. Rated 4.6/5 from over 3,000 reviews.

Product link: ikea.com — BILLY Bookcase

6. Basketball Hoop — Relocated, Not Removed

The basketball hoop moves to the open bottom-right floor space where it has more clearance and doesn’t interfere with the new setup.
